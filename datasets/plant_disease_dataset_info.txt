PlantVillage Dataset Information for कृषिVision
=================================================
Source: PlantVillage Dataset (CrowdAI / Penn State University benchmark)
Reference Archive: ml/plant_disease/dataset.zip (85.2 MB)
Total Images in Split: 54,305 images across train, validation, and test splits.

Supported 27 Focused Minor Project Classes:
- Tomato (10 classes): Bacterial Spot, Early Blight, Late Blight, Leaf Mold, Septoria Leaf Spot, Two-Spotted Spider Mites, Target Spot, Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus, Tomato Mosaic Virus, Healthy.
- Potato (3 classes): Early Blight, Late Blight, Healthy.
- Corn / Maize (4 classes): Cercospora Gray Leaf Spot, Common Rust, Northern Leaf Blight, Healthy.
- Apple (4 classes): Apple Scab, Black Rot, Cedar Apple Rust, Healthy.
- Grape (4 classes): Black Rot, Esca (Black Measles), Leaf Blight (Isariopsis), Healthy.
- Bell Pepper (2 classes): Bacterial Spot, Healthy.

Scripts Provided in backend/app/ml/plant_disease/:
- prepare_dataset.py: Splits raw color images into 70/15/15 train/val/test splits.
- verify_dataset.py: Performs directory and integrity audits across splits.
- test_dataloader.py: TensorFlow/Keras dataloader benchmark with MobileNetV2 preprocessing.
