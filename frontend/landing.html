Plant Leaf Disease Detection & 3-Tier Treatment Protocols

Early detection of plant pathologies is vital to preventing devastating crop yield loss. कृषिVision features an image processing pipeline resizing and normalizing leaf photos to 224×224 RGB tensors, supported by an authoritative 27-class agronomic encyclopedia.
